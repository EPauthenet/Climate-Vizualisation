# Climate-Vizualisation
Derived and inspired from : https://sites.ecmwf.int/data/c3sci/era5-daily/data/

The extrapolar surface ocean (60S-60N) is getting warmer at a rate of 0.012°C per year.

During the second year of ENSO events, the SST decrease at a rate of -0.27°C per year on average on the following ENSO events 82-83, 97-98, 09-10 and 15-16. For the year 2024, it is decreasing at a faster rate of -1.11°C per year (as of the 23rd of February).
